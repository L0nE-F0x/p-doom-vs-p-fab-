This is

A scoreboard for a disagreement.

Doomers and accelerationists already made opposite claims about jobs, energy, growth, and control. This page keeps score against those claims, from t0 = 22 March 2023.

This is not

A probability of the end of the world.

We borrowed the notation for the title. We do not get to borrow its authority. The needle is never shown as a percentage, and it makes no claim about how this ends.

Rule one

An indicator only ships if the two camps would have bet opposite ways on it.

Frontier benchmark scores don’t qualify — both sides predicted those would climb. Datacenter buildout doesn’t qualify — both predicted that too. What qualifies is where they actually disagreed: jobs, electricity bills, whether the boom shows up in productivity, whether anyone stays in control.

How to read the board
The gauge
43 p(doom)57 p(fab)

Two integers. They add to 100. There is no % sign, on purpose. Left is doom, right is fab. The needle is who is ahead on the record so far.

p(fab)

The probability that it goes fabulously right. Named for the fabs, because that is what the other side is building.

Fizzle is a third camp

A productivity miss refutes p(fab). It does not confirm p(doom). That track is scored separately and never mixed into the gauge.

Stale drops out

If a source goes quiet, its confidence decays and it leaves the tilt. We do not freeze a dead reading in place.

Rule two

No quote, no render.

Every indicator needs a named person, a date, a URL, and a verbatim quote on both poles. An empty slot does not render — the build fails. Paraphrase is how a headline gets laundered into a claim. If we can’t open the source, it stays unverified.

1

Change since t0

Unemployment at 4.4 scores nothing by itself. Unemployment versus March 2023 is the bet. t0 is 22 March 2023, the pause letter.

2

Categories, not piles

Three jobs series cannot outvote one energy series. We average inside a category first, then across categories.

3

Anchors are frozen

Where a claim has a number, that number is the pole. Changing an anchor later takes a pull request, and history keeps the old version.

What qualifies

Both camps have to have made opposite bets. Shared predictions are rejected, even if they would make a prettier needle.
